Design Philosophy

The Swift-Footed Achilles

In Homer’s Iliad, the hero Achilles is celebrated as "swift-footed" (πόδας ὠκὺς Ἀχιλλεύς)—embodying razor-sharp agility, unhindered momentum, and pure velocity. In an era where writing tools are weighed down by heavy web runtimes and sluggish electron wrappers, Akileas reclaims that effortless speed: immediate keystroke response, quiet simplicity, and a canvas that gets out of your way.

σευάμενος ὥς θ᾽ ἵππος ἀεθλοφόρος σὺν ὄχεσφιν,
ὅς ῥά τε ῥεῖα θέῃσι τιταινόμενος πεδίοιο·
ὣς Ἀχιλεὺς λαιψηρὰ πόδας καὶ γούνατ᾽ ἐνώμα.

“As a prize-winning steed with its chariot sweeps lightly over the plain in full career,
so Achilles swiftly plied his nimble feet and knees.”

— Homer, The Iliad, Book XXII (lines 22–24)

Latency-First Architecture

The UI Thread Never Waits.

Latency is treated as an uncompromising invariant. Keystrokes commit lightweight atomic edits to an in-memory Rope and immediately re-render a pending projection; full syntax re-parsing, layout calculation, and persistence converge asynchronously in background worker threads.

Three Orthogonal Pipelines

Formulas, diagrams, and tables compute independently. LaTeX formulas debounce updates; Mermaid diagrams compile on-demand with viewport caching; tables adaptively measure column widths. Editing prose never triggers costly diagram recalculations.

Formula Snapshots Mermaid Viewport Cache Adaptive Tables

Heterogeneous CPU & GPU Separation

Clear separation of concerns eliminates cross-domain overhead. The CPU manages Rope piece-tree transactions, hit-testing, incremental Tree-sitter slicing, and SIMD statistics; the GPU manages glyph atlases, layer compositing, and vector rasterization.

Rope Piece-Tree Glyph Shaping Cache GPU Hardware Acceleration

Resilient Crash Recovery

Protecting your thoughts against sudden power loss. A built-in time machine retains up to 100 historical snapshots with offline diffing, paired with an atomic binary AKREC001 crash journal that restores work immediately upon restart.

100-Snapshot History Binary Recovery Log Atomic Swap
