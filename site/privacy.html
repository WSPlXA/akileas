At a glance

What you should know

Documents stay localMarkdown files and folders are read and written on your device.
No Akileas accountThe app does not require registration or collect account profiles.
No developer telemetryWSPlXA does not receive analytics, document content, or usage history from the app.

01 · Local processing

Information handled on your device

Akileas processes the documents and folders you choose to open, text you paste, editing history needed during the current session, and application preferences. This processing is required to edit, render, search, save, and recover your work.

Preferences and bounded crash-recovery data may be stored locally so the application can restore settings or unsaved work. You control those files through your device and operating-system storage controls.

02 · Data collection

What WSPlXA receives

Akileas does not transmit your document content, filenames, workspace contents, clipboard data, preferences, or usage analytics to WSPlXA. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

03 · Network access

When the app may connect elsewhere

If a Markdown document references an image with an http:// or https:// address, Akileas may request that image from the host named in the address so it can be displayed. That third-party host may receive standard request information such as your IP address and user-agent details under its own privacy policy.

When you choose to open an external link, your default browser handles the destination. Akileas does not control the privacy practices of external sites.

04 · Store distribution

Microsoft Store data

If you obtain Akileas through Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process purchase, installation, reliability, and Store account information as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. WSPlXA does not receive your document content through Microsoft Store.

05 · Children

Children's privacy

Akileas is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. Because the app does not operate an account service or collect data for WSPlXA, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

06 · Changes

Policy updates

If Akileas changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be described in the project's release notes when practical.

Questions

Contact the publisher

Privacy and support requests can be submitted publicly through the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not include private document content or other sensitive information in an issue.
