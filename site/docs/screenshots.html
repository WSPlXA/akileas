Chapter IV

Interface Tour

From focused writing and side-by-side comparison to formulas, diagrams, version rollback and publication-grade export, the eight screens below cover the everyday Akileas workflow. Each one explains what it solves, how to open it, and what you will see.

4.1 About These Screens

Akileas keeps its interface deliberately restrained: no redundant toolbars, no panels that keep flipping in and out. While you write, it all but fades into the background; when you need a particular capability, you summon it with a single shortcut and dismiss it when you are done.

4.2 The Eight Core Screens

Main Editor

What it does:This is where you will spend most of your time. Markdown syntax expands only in the block the cursor is in; move away and it immediately renders into typeset form — headings, quotes, code blocks and checkboxes all take on their final appearance right where they are.

What you'll see:A clean dark layout with clearly ranked headings, block quotes carrying a soft rule along the side, and task lists shown as clickable boxes. There is no left/right split and no preview pane.

Split View

What it does:Opens the same document in two viewports at once, so you can edit on one side while watching the overall result on the other; you can also open two different documents in the two viewports for reference comparison. Both sides share a single body of content, so typing on the left updates the right within milliseconds.

How to open it:Menu Document → Split Right (side by side) or Split Down (stacked). Use Ctrl + H / J / K / L to move focus between the viewports.

What you'll see:Two viewports that scroll independently and each keep their own cursor position, while the content always stays identical.

Mermaid Diagrams

What it does:Write flowcharts, sequence diagrams, state diagrams, class diagrams and mind maps directly in the document, and Akileas renders them as vector graphics. Grouping containers and wrapped labels are supported, and you can zoom with the mouse wheel or hold and drag to pan.

What you'll see:The diagram is drawn as vectors, so lines stay sharp at any magnification; when a diagram is scaled down, a local magnifier appears automatically so that dense nodes and connections remain easy to read.

Math Formulas

What it does:Write inline and block formulas using LaTeX syntax, with support for common expressions including integrals, matrices, systems of equations, superscripts and subscripts, and radicals.

What you'll see:Formulas are centred in print-quality typesetting with smooth glyph outlines that show no jagged edges when magnified — Greek letters and the full range of mathematical symbols included.

Time Machine

What it does:Recover earlier versions without configuring Git. Every save automatically leaves a snapshot behind, up to 100 of them; you can also press Ctrl + Shift + S to place a version anchor by hand.

How to open it:Press Ctrl + Shift + H.

What you'll see:A version timeline on the left and, on the right, a character-by-character difference between the selected version and the current content — green for additions, red for deletions — with one-click restore at any time.

Outline and Find

What it does:Fast jumping and searching in long documents. Ctrl + D summons the outline tree, listing every heading level; Ctrl + F finds and Ctrl + H replaces.

What you'll see:The outline highlights the section you are currently in as you scroll, and clicking an entry jumps there smoothly; matches are highlighted in the text, and Enter moves through them. Find also supports whole-word matching, case sensitivity and regular expressions.

Agent Configuration Workbench

What it does:When you write agent configuration files such as SKILL.md and AGENTS.md, Akileas switches to a dedicated mode automatically and checks whether the metadata at the top is complete: whether any required fields are missing, whether the name matches, and whether the version number is correctly formatted.

What you'll see:A lightweight diagnostics panel floats up at the bottom of the interface, listing each problem in turn; click the fix button and the missing fields or template content are filled in for you.

PDF Export

What it does:Exports the document as a PDF you can print or submit for publication. Body text, mathematical formulas and diagrams are all embedded as vectors, so they stay sharp when magnified.

How to open it:Menu File → Export as PDF….

What you'll see:An export settings dialog where you can choose A4 or US Letter paper, portrait or landscape orientation, page margins, and whether to print headers, footers and page numbers.
